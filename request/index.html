How it works

  1. Fill in the form. Course, test date, the topic list, and where you are on each topic. A graded quiz or homework with the marks still on it helps more than anything else, if your course lets you share it.
  2. I reply by e-mail within 2 days with a yes, or with what's missing.
  3. I build it and check it against your course, then send you a link.
  4. The day after your test, I ask whether it helped. That's how the next one gets better.

When to ask

0–2 days

Too late to help well. I'll say so.

3–6 days

A one-page recall sheet.

7+ days before

The full set: practice, solutions, and a plan.

Material crammed into the last night doesn't stick, and I won't pretend it does.

The rules

  • Study material only. I don't write, solve, or check anything you turn in for a grade.
  • Written fresh. Your instructor's slides, exams and answer keys are never copied or passed along.
  • Made with AI tools, then checked by me before it reaches you.
  • Free. Nothing is sold.
  • Send only what your course lets you share. That includes a graded quiz, slides or an old exam. If your instructor doesn't allow sharing it, send your topic list instead.
  • Your course's rules about outside help still apply, and following them is on you.

At ODU these lines follow the Code of Student Conduct, Policy 1530 (revised 2024-06-14), §VIII, checked 2026-09-15. Other schools: your own code wins.

What the form asks

Title
Your course and the test, like "MATH 211 — Exam 2".
Topic list
Paste it from your syllabus, study guide or review sheet.
Where you are now
For each topic: never seen it · can't do it · can do it slowly · solid. Guessing high gets you material that wastes your time.
Format
How the test runs: closed book, calculator allowed, timed, and so on.
Graded work
A photo or PDF of a quiz or homework you got back, marks showing, if your course lets you share it. Optional.
Course materials · Materials link
Your syllabus or topic sheet. If you use a link, set it so anyone with the link can view. Optional.
Study time available
Minutes a day, and the days you already can't study.

What happens to what you send

  • The form asks for your name, e-mail, school, course, test date and topic list, and anything you choose to upload.
  • It is stored in my Notion workspace (Notion runs the form). To build and check your material, what you send also passes through the AI services I work with, such as Anthropic's Claude and OpenAI's ChatGPT. It is never posted publicly or sold.
  • Uploads are kept one semester, then deleted.
  • Your request record (name, e-mail, school, course, dates) is kept until one year after your test date, then deleted.
  • Ask any time and I'll delete yours sooner, uploads included.
