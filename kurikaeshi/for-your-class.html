Kurikaeshi for your class

A short guide for teachers. Updated October 2026.

Kurikaeshi is a free flashcard app. It brings each word back just before a student forgets it, a few minutes a day, on a phone or a laptop, and it keeps working offline.

It is an independent app, not run by your school. Students choose to use it, and nothing in it is a grade.

Give students a deck

  1. Send students the link for your course from the table below.
  2. They tap Sign in. It is free and takes about a minute.
  3. They choose the lesson the class is on. New cards come from that lesson and the ones before it, a few a day.

Need a deck for another course? Send a spreadsheet with the columns word, reading, meaning and lesson, and it will be added with its own link.

Run a class

A class is optional. It lets you see how students are doing.

  1. Sign in, then tap You, then Classes, then Create a class. Enter a title and the last day of the term.
  2. Hand out the six-character code, or tap Share class link.
  3. Students open the link, or enter the code under You, then Classes. They see exactly what they would share and agree to it.
  4. Tap Approve for each student on your roster.
  5. Add the course deck on your own phone, then open the class and tap Publish deck.

See how the class is doing

Open the class and tap Open the class view. It shows:

  • the cards the class misses most, to bring to class
  • how far students are through each lesson
  • who has not studied in the last seven days
  • which version of the deck each student has

Copy report copies it as text.

It shows only what each student's phone reported, so treat it like a handed-in worksheet: a record of practice, not a grade.

Good to know

  • The class ends by itself after its last day. That date cannot be changed later; a new term is a new class.
  • Leaving is instant. A student who leaves, or whom you remove, drops out of the class view at once, including what they shared before.
  • Only signed-in students appear. A student studying from a deck file, without an account, is not in the class view.
  • Your own cards. Tap Write my own cards, then Share deck to send them by link. Cards you wrote yourself cannot be published to a class.
  • Flip through. Tap a deck or lesson, then Flip through to show the cards one by one, like paper flashcards. It changes nobody's schedule.
  • Use the browser. A copy added to the home screen cannot sign in, so students should sign in and study in their browser.
  • Without accounts. A student can export a progress file (You, Settings, Export my progress) and choose to give it to you. Read it under You, Settings, For whoever teaches the course, Read student exports. Nothing leaves your device.

Privacy

  • Joining a class shares one thing: the student's reviews of that class's decks (which card, when, how it went).
  • You never see a password or an e-mail address. Your roster shows the name each student chose.
  • A student can leave a class with one tap, or delete their account under You, Delete account.

Readings are checked against a named dictionary, not typed from memory. Sources and licences are listed in the app under About Kurikaeshi.
