# Study Kit

Free study sheets for the Japanese courses at Old Dominion University, built from the
assigned lessons and nothing else. Every sheet reads on a phone and prints as a handout.

**Live:** <https://ferrelldevin.github.io/studykit/>

## What's here

| Sheet | Course | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| [Say It First](japn301/say-it-first/) | JAPN 301 · Irodori A2-2 L.1 | The whole six-line self-introduction written out, with ready-made options under every slot. Speaking becomes reading aloud. |
| [Kanji Quiz Prep](japn309/kanji-quiz-prep/) | JAPN 309 · Lessons 1–4 | All 64 characters of the first kanji quiz. Cold sheet first, then tap-to-reveal drills for the misses. |
| [Writing the Thirty-Two](japn309/writing-the-thirty-two/) | JAPN 309 · Lessons 3–4 | The 32 characters drilled for handwriting: stroke counts, component builds, and the three pairs that cost marks. |
| [Kurikaeshi](kurikaeshi/) | JAPN 309 · Lessons 1–8 | A spaced-repetition scheduler, not a sheet: 128 characters and their dictionary words, resurfaced on the day you would forget them. Installs to a phone home screen and works offline; your progress stays in your own browser. |

## The rules this repo runs on

Read [CONVENTIONS.md](CONVENTIONS.md) before editing anything. The load-bearing one: this
repo is public, so it carries **course-general material only**. Anything built for a named
learner lives in a private Drive folder shared to that person, never here.

## Sources

Kanji readings and stroke counts are checked against KANJIDIC2 (© EDRDG, used under the
EDRDG licence), not typed from memory. Japanese sentences quoted as models come from the
courses' own textbooks and are labelled where they appear.
