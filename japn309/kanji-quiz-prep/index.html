Step 3 · The pairs that get marked wrong

母 毎mother / every — 毎 has a lid on top, 母 does not
力 刀strength / sword — the sword leans; strength stands square
門 間gate / interval — 間 has the sun sitting inside the gate
土 士earth (L.2) vs the samurai lookalike — pairs up properly in L.6, worth knowing now
Every one of these has been missed by somebody who knew both characters perfectly well. Thirty seconds each, tonight.
